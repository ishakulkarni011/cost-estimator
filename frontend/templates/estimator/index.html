New to this? How the estimate works
Tax equalisation
The employee should be no better and no worse off than if they had stayed at home. The employer pays whatever tax the host country charges.
Hypothetical tax
The tax and social security the employee would have paid at home. The employer deducts it from their pay, so they still bear their usual home tax. It reduces the employer's cost.
Tax on tax
UK tax paid by the employer is itself taxable income, so it has to be grossed up. The tool repeats the calculation until the tax stops changing, solving T = UKTax(I + T) where I is net home pay plus taxable benefits.
Social security
Which country's system applies depends on the social security agreement between the UK and Switzerland. The outcome is explained under the results.
